Welcome to my web page!
<p align="justify">
I am currently a lecturer in applied mathematics (enseignant-chercheur, equivalent to "maître de conférence") at <a href="https://www.isae-supaero.fr/en/">ISAE-Supaéro</a>  since 2024. Between 2023 and 2024 I was a post doc at the <a href="https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/en/">Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse (IMT)</a> under the supervision of Adrien Mazoyer and Fabrice Gamboa, working on multivariate conformal inference.</p>
<p align="justify">
 I pursued my PhD in 2023 at <a href="https://www.insa-toulouse.fr/en/">INSA Toulouse</a>/IMT, under the supervision of Pascal Noble (IMT/INSA) and Olivier Roustant (IMT/INSA). The PhD was funded by the Service Hydrographique et Océanographique de la Marine (SHOM), in collaboration with Rémy Baraille (SHOM) in particular.</p>
<p align="justify">
The PhD was motivated by applying machine learning techniques to oceanography, coastal marine forecast and more broadly to physical models described by PDEs.</p>

Prior to my PhD I spent some years on the Plateau de Saclay.
